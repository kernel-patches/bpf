Subject: [PATCH] bpf: Exit early if reg_bounds_sync gets invalid inputs

This commit adds an early exit to reg_bounds_sync, in case its inputs
are invalid (e.g. umin > umax).

Because reg_bounds_sync now preserves invalid inputs, we can eliminate
extra calls to range_bounds_violation in simulate_both_branches.
